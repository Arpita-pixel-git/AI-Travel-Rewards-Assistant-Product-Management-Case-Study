# 11 — Product Prototype

## Prototype Overview

The prototype demonstrates the end-to-end experience of an AI-powered travel rewards assistant.

The experience focuses on helping users move from understanding their rewards to achieving a specific travel goal.

## Key Screens

### 1. Rewards Dashboard

Provides a unified view of the user's reward portfolio and available points.

### 2. Travel Goal

Allows users to define a specific travel aspiration such as a destination, trip, or cabin preference.

### 3. Goal Progress

Shows how the user's existing rewards contribute toward the selected travel goal.

### 4. AI Recommendation

Presents personalized redemption opportunities based on the user's rewards, travel goal, and preferences.

### 5. Redemption Opportunity

Shows the recommended redemption path, estimated value, points required, and relevant alternatives.

## Design Direction

The prototype uses a dark, premium interface with glassmorphism-inspired UI to align with the financial + travel positioning of the product.

## Prototype Objective

The goal of the prototype is not simply to demonstrate visual design.

It demonstrates the product hypothesis:

> **Can AI turn fragmented reward information into a simple, confident travel decision?**
