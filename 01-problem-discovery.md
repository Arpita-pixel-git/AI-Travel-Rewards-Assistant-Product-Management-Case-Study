# 01 — Problem Discovery

## Problem

Travel reward points are often scattered across multiple credit cards, banks, airlines, and hotel loyalty programs. Users may have significant reward value but struggle to understand how to use it effectively.

Users often struggle to answer questions such as:

- Which card should I use for this purchase?
- How much are my points actually worth?
- Should I transfer points to an airline or hotel partner?
- What is the best way to use my points for a specific trip?
- Do I have enough points for my desired redemption?

The core opportunity is to move users from **information discovery to personalized decision-making**.

## Problem Statement

How might we help travel-reward users understand the value of their fragmented rewards and make confident, personalized redemption decisions without requiring them to manually research multiple programs?

## Opportunity

An AI-powered experience can bring together reward balances, travel goals, user preferences, and redemption options to provide actionable recommendations rather than simply displaying information.

**User goal → AI analysis → Redemption options → Best recommendation**
