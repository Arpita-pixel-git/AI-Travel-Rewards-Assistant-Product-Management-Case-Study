# 04 — Personas & Jobs-to-be-Done

## Primary Persona

### The Value-Seeking Traveler

A credit-card user who actively earns reward points and wants to maximize their value for flights and hotels.

### Goals

- Maximize reward value
- Reduce travel costs
- Achieve specific travel goals
- Avoid wasting points
- Make confident redemption decisions

### Frustrations

- Too many redemption options
- Complex transfer rules
- Unclear point valuations
- Information spread across multiple platforms
- Lack of personalized guidance

## Jobs-to-be-Done

### Functional Job

> When I am planning a trip, I want to understand the best way to use my available reward points so that I can maximize their value.

### Emotional Job

> I want to feel confident that I am not wasting my hard-earned points.

### Social Job

> I want to feel smart and informed about how I use my travel rewards.

## Key User Need

Users don't just need access to reward information.

They need **confidence in the decision they make with that information.**
