# 07 — AI Product Design

## AI Use Case

The AI layer acts as a decision-support engine that converts reward data and travel intent into personalized recommendations.

## AI Workflow

**User Intent → Data Retrieval → Analysis → Ranking → Explanation**

### 1. Understand Intent

Identify:

- Destination
- Dates
- Travel preferences
- Cabin preference
- Number of travelers
- User priorities

### 2. Retrieve Information

Retrieve relevant:

- Reward balances
- Transfer partners
- Redemption options
- Point requirements
- Relevant restrictions

### 3. Analyze Options

Evaluate potential redemption paths based on:

- Point cost
- Monetary value
- Transfer requirements
- Availability
- User preferences

### 4. Rank Options

Rank recommendations based on a weighted decision framework.

### 5. Explain

Provide a concise explanation of:

- Recommended option
- Expected value
- Why it fits the user's goal
- Alternatives

## AI Product Principle

AI should **augment the user's decision-making**, not make an opaque decision on the user's behalf.
