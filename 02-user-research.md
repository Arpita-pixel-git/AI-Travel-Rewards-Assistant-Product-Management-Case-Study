# 02 — User Research

## Research Objective

Understand how travel-reward users currently manage points, evaluate redemption options, and make travel-related reward decisions.

## Key Research Questions

- How do users currently track their reward points?
- What makes reward redemption difficult?
- What information do users need before making a redemption decision?
- Where do users spend the most time researching?
- What causes users to lose confidence in a recommendation?
- What would make users trust an AI-powered reward assistant?

## Key User Pain Points

### 1. Fragmented information
Reward balances and redemption options are distributed across different cards, banks, airlines, and hotel programs.

### 2. Complex redemption decisions
Users need to compare multiple transfer partners, redemption values, availability, and restrictions.

### 3. Lack of personalization
Generic reward information does not account for the user's specific travel goal, points balance, preferences, or urgency.

### 4. Unclear point value
Users often don't know whether a redemption represents good or poor value.

### 5. Research fatigue
Finding the best redemption can require significant manual research across multiple platforms.

## Research Insight

The problem is not simply **"finding reward information."**

The deeper problem is:

> **Users need help deciding what to do with their rewards.**

This insight drives the product strategy toward personalized decision support rather than another rewards information dashboard.
