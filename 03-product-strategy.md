# 03 — Product Strategy

## Product Vision

Help every travel-reward user turn fragmented points into meaningful travel experiences through personalized, intelligent recommendations.

## Product Strategy

The product moves through three layers:

**Aggregate → Understand → Recommend**

### Aggregate
Bring reward balances and relevant information into one place.

### Understand
Understand the user's travel goals, preferences, points, and constraints.

### Recommend
Compare available redemption paths and recommend the best option.

## Target User

Primary users are credit-card users who actively collect travel rewards and want to maximize their value but find redemption decisions complex or time-consuming.

## Core Product Bet

If users receive personalized and explainable reward recommendations based on their actual travel goals, they will be more likely to engage with their rewards and complete higher-value redemptions.

## Product Principles

1. **Value over volume** — focus on actionable insights rather than more information.
2. **Explainability** — users should understand why a recommendation was made.
3. **Personalization** — recommendations should reflect individual goals and preferences.
4. **Trust first** — AI recommendations must be grounded in reliable reward data.
5. **Progressive intelligence** — start simple and introduce proactive AI capabilities over time.
